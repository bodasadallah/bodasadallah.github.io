<h2 class="dn-section">Research and techniques</h2>

<div class="dn-item">
<h3><a href="https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1xjnz40/multitoken_prediction_done_right_r/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Multi-Token Prediction Done Right [R]</a></h3>
<div class="dn-meta">r/MachineLearning · Oct 30</div>
<p>The Soft-MTP preprint argues that the standard multi-token prediction gradient contains a useful KL self-distillation signal hidden behind noise that hurts next-token prediction. The authors recover that signal with a one-line gradient trick and report gains across tasks. Code and checkpoints are released.</p>
</div>

<div class="dn-item">
<h3><a href="https://latent.space/p/elorian/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Can Frontier Models See Past a Decoy? Elorian Tests Visual Reasoning</a></h3>
<div class="dn-meta">Latent Space · Oct 29</div>
<p>Elorian has a new decoy-counting benchmark that it says exposes a gap between what frontier models see and how they reason visually. Its Glimpse model tries to close that gap by "thinking with images". The general lesson about decoy-based evaluation design is the relevant part for you.</p>
</div>

<div class="dn-item">
<h3><a href="https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/blog/from-synthetic-enterprise-data-evaluations-to-real-microsoft-copilot-ai-confidence/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">From synthetic enterprise data evaluations to real Microsoft Copilot AI confidence</a></h3>
<div class="dn-meta">Microsoft Research · Oct 30</div>
<p>Microsoft Research describes how it builds synthetic enterprise data to evaluate Copilot when real data is too sensitive to use. It is relevant to evaluation methodology whenever the realistic test data is confidential, as review data often is.</p>
</div>

<div class="dn-item">
<h3><a href="https://openai.com/index/updated-model-spec" target="_blank" rel="noopener">An updated Model Spec for more capable and beneficial AI</a></h3>
<div class="dn-meta">OpenAI · Oct 29</div>
<p>OpenAI has updated its Model Spec around five core values, including sound judgment, human agency and faithfulness to human intent. It is a reference point for the norms that LLM judges and LLM reviewers are supposed to follow.</p>
