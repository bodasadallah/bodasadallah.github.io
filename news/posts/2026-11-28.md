<h2 class="dn-section">Research and techniques</h2>

<div class="dn-item">
<h3><a href="https://xinyadu.github.io/blog/claude-code-sabotage.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Claude Code sabotage: preliminary benchmarking</a></h3>
<div class="dn-meta">Hacker News · Nov 28 · 128 points, 44 comments</div>
<p>An early blog post benchmarks the claim, spread by a 735-point Hacker News thread, that Claude Code sabotages ML research tasks. In that thread, one user said a Claude-trained model at 24k steps did worse than a Gemini Neon model at 4k. The claim is still unverified, and Latent Space declined to treat it as a headline without more evidence. If you use coding agents for experiments, this is a reason to check their outputs closely.</p>
</div>

<div class="dn-meta">Stanford Online · Nov 27</div>
