<div class="dn-lede">This period, about 21 hours since the last digest, is dominated by the Riemann Hypothesis. Levent Alpöge claims that he and Claude proved the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis and formalized the proof, and the Lean and Mathlib communities have put out a joint statement on verifying it. Separately, Alessandro Masullo has posted an all-human GRH proof that Brian Conrad has been checking for months. Beyond that: Anthropic listed on Thursday at about $2 trillion while the Nasdaq slid, and Thinking Machines' Inkling-2-Mini drew thousands of likes on Hugging Face.</div>

<h2 class="dn-section">Big news</h2>

<div class="dn-item">
<h3><a href="https://alpo.ge/remark.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Levent Alpöge claims proof of the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis [pdf]</a></h3>
<div class="dn-meta">Hacker News · Nov 20 · 2876 points, 2058 comments</div>
<p>Levent Alpöge claims in a short PDF that, working with Claude, he found a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis that extends to the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis, and that he and Claude formalized it without assumptions. For now it is his own claim; it is the most discussed story on Hacker News, with 2,876 points and over 2,000 comments.</p>
</div>

<div class="dn-item">
<h3><a href="https://leanprover-community.github.io/blog/posts/riemann-hypothesis/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Lean verification of the Riemann Hypothesis</a></h3>
<div class="dn-meta">Hacker News · Nov 21 · 1032 points, 851 comments</div>
<p>The Lean and Mathlib communities, backed by the Lean FRO and the Mathlib Initiative, issued a joint statement on the Lean verification of Alpöge's Riemann Hypothesis proof, released November 20. This is the community check on the AI-assisted claim, and a clear case of formal verification standing in for slow human review of a high-stakes result.</p>
</div>

<div class="dn-item">
<h3><a href="https://math216.wordpress.com/2026/11/20/hurried-announcement-alessandro-masullo-grh/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Hurried Announcement: Alessandro Masullo GRH</a></h3>
<div class="dn-meta">Hacker News · Nov 20 · 2416 points, 1175 comments</div>
<p>Ravi Vakil announced in a hurry that Alessandro Masullo has a proposed all-human proof of GRH, with no AI involved. Brian Conrad has studied it closely for months and says it looks good so far. Its vetting is still under way: the plan had been to polish the writing and run it past more people before announcing, and the draft came out early because of Alpöge's announcement.</p>
</div>

<div class="dn-item">
<h3><a href="https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/11/20/unforced-euler-singularity-unforced-navier-stokes-singularity-and-the-generalized-riemann-hypothesis/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Unforced Navier-Stokes Singularity and the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis</a></h3>
