<h2 class="dn-section">Big news</h2>

<div class="dn-item">
<h3><a href="https://latent.space/p/ainews-math-proofs-are-landing-faster-than-we-can-read-them/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">[AINews] Math Proofs Are Landing Faster Than We Can Read Them</a></h3>
<div class="dn-meta">Latent Space · Nov 25</div>
<p>Latent Space reports an OpenAI preprint claiming that the square matrix multiplication exponent ω is exactly 9/4. That would follow October's upper bound of 9/4 with a matching lower bound. The same newsletter counts 64 AI-assisted research results this year, notes that some proofs are still under review, and says OpenAI withdrew three papers in October after a sign error. That makes it a useful case study in how fast machine-generated scholarship is outpacing review.</p>
