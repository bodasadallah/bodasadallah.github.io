<h3><a href="https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/23/glm-54/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">GLM-5.4: Toward Generalized Coding with Our First Loop Transformer</a></h3>
<div class="dn-meta">Simon Willison · Nov 23</div>
<p>Z.ai released GLM-5.4, its first looped transformer: a group of weight-shared layers runs several times per pass, giving a deeper network without more parameters. <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32778" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Beyond Fixed-Context Likelihood: Zero-Shot AI-Generated Text Detection via Context Perturbation Discrepancy</a></h3>
<div class="dn-why">New zero-shot AI-generated text detector, applicable to detecting AI-written reviews and papers.</div>
<figure><img src="https://arxiv.org/html/2611.32778v1/figure1.png" alt="Main figure of Beyond Fixed-Context Likelihood: Zero-Shot AI-Generated Text Detection via Context Perturbation Discrepancy" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Figure 1: Fixed-context likelihood (top) and CPD (bottom). Left: token likelihood versus contextual response deviation. Right: document score distributions on HART for (a) direct detection (H vs. A) and (b) detection by draft origin under editing (H/HA vs. A/AH). H and A denote original human and AI</figcaption></figure>
<details><summary>Abstract</summary><p>The same increase in token log probability from earlier context can be typical at one position and unusually large at another. We introduce Context Perturbation Discrepancy (CPD), a zero-shot detector that measures this gain relative to what a single pretrained language model expects. Keeping the passage fixed, CPD scores each observed token under its full prefix and under a short window of recent tokens. Under the full-prefix distribution, the expected gain equals the KL divergence from that distribution to the restricted-prefix distribution. CPD subtracts this expectation from each observed gain and scales the sum of the signed deviations by its analytical reference standard deviation. On unedited human and AI texts from GEN, DetectRL-X, and HART, subtracting the expected gain raises AUROC by 32.78-35.81 percentage points at the same variance scale. CPD outperforms seven zero-shot baselines, averaging 94.80% AUROC and 79.60% recall at a 5% false-positive rate. On original and edited HART passages, the same scores separate texts more clearly by draft origin than by the benchmark's final-expression labels (89.15% versus 65.50% AUROC). Code is available at https://github.com/BUPT-tyf/CPD.</p></details>
